Support

Using your gallery

Open your gallery and press Select under any photograph. Selected frames are outlined in gold. Use the Selected filter at the top to review your picks, then press Submit selection to send them to the studio.

If downloads are enabled for your gallery you will see a small arrow button on each photograph, plus Download all in the toolbar which delivers a ZIP archive of the full set.

Nothing is ever deleted. Email the studio and we will reopen the archive for you within a day.

Ask the studio to switch your gallery to shareable link. You will then receive a private URL and an access code you can pass on, without sharing your login.
